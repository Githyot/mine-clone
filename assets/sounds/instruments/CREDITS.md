# Instrument recordings: credits and licence

All files here are cut from the **VS Chamber Orchestra: Community Edition** by Versilian
Studios (recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell, sample cutting by Elan Hickler),
<https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE>, released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Nothing comes from Minecraft.

They were converted to mono 22.05 kHz, trimmed to 1.2–3 s with a fade-out, low-passed at 7 kHz
and normalised. The game retunes each note from the nearest recording (mods/melodies.rs).
